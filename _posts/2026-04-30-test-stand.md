WIP

<p style="font-size:1.2em;">
In order to validate and characterize NYU Rogue Aerospace's first hybrid propulsion system, 
my senior design team and I created Zuko, a modular static fire test stand.
</p>

## Project Requirements
- test stand shall withstand all structural, vibrational, and thermal loading experienced during a static fire
- test stand shall accommodate propulsion systems up to 10" in diameter and 8' in length
- thrust data from load cell shall be transmitted wirelessly and able to be monitored live
- test stand shall be able to be assembled/disassembled in under 30 min
